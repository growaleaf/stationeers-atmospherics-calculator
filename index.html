Stationeers — Atmospherics Gas-Mix Calculator

Enter your room or suit volume, target pressure, and temperature for Stationeers, set a gas mix, and get the moles — and the STP-liter equivalent — of each gas needed to reach it. The ideal-gas-law constant and the oxygen breathing bands below are each confirmed against two independently fetched sources. Pollutant and volatile exposure limits are not built in as defaults — the only number either had was single-sourced, so those are fields you read off your own in-game atmospherics analyzer. Nothing here is saved or sent anywhere; the math runs in your browser.

Formulas & known-answer checks

n_total = (P[kPa]·1000)·V[L] / (R·T[K]), then n_gas = n_total · fraction_gas. STP-equivalent liters use IUPAC STP (273.15 K, 100 kPa), derived from the same R rather than a separate hardcoded molar volume: V_STP = n_gas · R · 273.15 / 100000.

How this differs from existing Stationeers tools

stationeersfurnace.com covers furnace/smelting recipes only — no life-support mixing. stationeers-wiki.com is a reference wiki with recipe and property pages, not an interactive calculator. A third tool found during this build, a Stationeers gas-mixer calculator (hosted at supercraft.host), solves the ideal gas law and a volatiles/oxygen fuel combustion ratio for engines — it has no breathable- atmosphere presets, no multi-gas life-support mix, and no safety-band reference info at all. This page is scoped to the different mechanic: mixing a room or suit atmosphere a colonist can breathe, not fuel for an engine or a furnace recipe.

Sources & last verified date

All citations below fetched directly 2026-10-08.

  1. stationeers-wiki.com — Pressure, Volume, Quantity, and Temperature: "P * V = n * R * T", R = "8314.46261815324" "(Lit * Pa)/(mol * deg K)", with a worked furnace example (2 mol, 300 K, 1000 L) solving to "≈5 kPa" — reproduced exactly in the Formulas section above.
  2. supercraft.host — Stationeers Gas Mixer Calculator: an independently built third-party calculator for the same game, whose ideal-gas-law panel uses "R = 8.314" (J/(mol·K)) — the same constant this page uses, in base SI units rather than liter-pascal units.
  3. stationeers-wiki.com — Air: "16 kPa or higher" O2 partial pressure is safe to breathe with no lung warning; "16 kPa to 12 kPa" gives an OXYGEN LOW warning; "12 kPa to 5 kPa" gives OXYGEN CRITICAL, in both cases with no damage stated on this page.
  4. xgamingserver.com — Stationeers Atmospherics 101: independently states players need "at least ~16 kPa O2" to remove a helmet without lung warnings, matching the wiki's 16 kPa floor without citing it.
  5. stationeers-wiki.com — EVA Suit: states a 10 L suit volume and a 202.65 kPa maximum suit pressure. Single-sourced; shown for reference only.
  6. stationeers-wiki.com — Pollutant: "A concentration of 0.1 Mol or greater will cause your suit to warn about pollutants/poison in the air." The same text is mirrored on this wiki's own legacy subdomain — one underlying source, not two — so it is not shipped as a built-in default.
  7. Steam Community discussion, Stationeers forum: states the atmosphere becomes explosive once both oxygen and volatiles exceed roughly 5% each, and ignites at 300°C or from a welder. Single community source, not wiki-confirmed; not used as a default — the welding-bypass preset above instead zeroes oxygen entirely, which needs no threshold number to be correct.
